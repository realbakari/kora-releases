Kora 1.0

The first release of Kora, a home on your Mac for a community’s photographs, recordings, flyers, newsletters and documents.

  • Bring it together. Import files and whole folders without changing your originals. Kora reads their details, makes previews, recognises duplicates by their contents and never removes one on its own.
  • Describe as you go. Titles, dates, places, creators, consent and notes, with a history of every change. A date nobody knows stays blank.
  • Organise. Collections and folders, tags, events, people and organisations, a timeline of your organisation’s history, and smart lists for what needs review.
  • Find anything. Search across details, documents and the text Kora reads in photos and scanned flyers.
  • Share what you’ve kept. Stories, exhibitions, annual reports, and exports that check consent first and leave GPS locations out unless you ask.
  • Write in Markdown. Reports, stories, exhibitions and descriptions take headings, lists, quotes and emphasis, styled as you type.
  • Optional, and off until you turn them on: an on-Mac assistant that only suggests, Kora in Spotlight, and iCloud sync between your own Macs.

No account and no analytics. Kora connects to the internet only to sync with your iCloud when you turn that on, and to check for updates.
